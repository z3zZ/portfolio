    The Ascendant

    The astronomer

    The magic is in the wielder.

    I believe the ethical weight of technology sits with whoever holds it, not the tool. So I try to hold it well.

    I came into software through games, modding Minecraft and Counter-Strike. Code still feels like LEGO: the fun is in the building. I chose a T Level over A Levels to start building sooner, and worked with Amazon Prime Video at seventeen. Along the way: shadowing at Tribeca in 2023, and Amazon’s Step Up work experience in 2025.

    I own what I ship, from the first line to the last bug, and I'm drawn to ambitious engineering, developer tools, AI and software that feels exceptional to use.
