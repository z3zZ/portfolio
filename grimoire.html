Chapter IOrigin

Origin

Every wizard starts with a game.

I found software through games: modding Minecraft and Counter-Strike, pulling things apart to see how they worked. Code still feels like LEGO to me. The fun is in the building, piece by piece, until it works and then works beautifully.

So I took a different road. A T Level in Digital Production, Design and Development instead of A Levels, finished with a Distinction, and a placement at Amazon Prime Video at seventeen. Along the way: shadowing at Tribeca in 2023, and Amazon’s Step Up work experience in 2025.

Excited by the idea. Satisfied by the result.

Some notes in this book are written in invisible ink. Move your cursor across a page to find them.

Chapter VIIArcanum

The wielder

The magic is in the wielder.

I believe the ethical weight of technology sits with whoever holds it, not the tool. So I try to hold it well.

That means owning what I ship, from the first line to the last bug. I'm drawn to ambitious engineering, developer tools, AI, and software that feels exceptional to use.

Now I'm a Junior Application Developer at Thirtyone:eight, a safeguarding charity going through real change, and studying Computer Science with AI at The Open University.

The clearest plans tend to arrive around 4:30 in the morning.
