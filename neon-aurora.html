A function is a formula. Get the words right and something real appears that wasn't there before. That's the part of engineering I enjoy most, so every project in the grimoire comes with its incantation.

Built for the night

I do my deepest work late, when it's quiet and the city is lit. So this whole site lives at night, and the night here is anything but dark.

Ownership
