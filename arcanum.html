Chapter I · Origin

Every wizard starts with a game.

I found software through games: modding Minecraft and Counter-Strike, pulling things apart to see how they worked. Code still feels like LEGO to me. The fun is in the building, piece by piece, until it works and then works beautifully.

So I took a different road. A T Level in Digital Production, Design and Development instead of A Levels, finished with a Distinction, with a placement at Amazon Prime Video at seventeen building real tools. Along the way: shadowing at Tribeca in 2023, and Amazon’s Step Up work experience in 2025.

Excited by the idea. Satisfied by the result.
